# Paid Time Off Policy — Employee Handbook (Rev. 2024)

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework under which full-time employees of Northbridge
Analytics accrue, request, and use Paid Time Off (PTO). It is intended to give team
members the flexibility to rest, travel, and attend to personal matters while ensuring
adequate coverage across all departments.

## 2. Eligibility

All full-time employees become eligible for PTO accrual on their first day of
employment. Part-time employees scheduled for fewer than 30 hours per week accrue PTO
on a pro-rated basis. Contractors and interns are not eligible under this policy.

## 3. Accrual Schedule

PTO accrues per completed pay period according to length of service:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO | Accrual per Pay Period |
|------------------|-----------|------------------------|
| 0–2 years        | 15 days   | 4.62 hours             |
| 3–5 years        | 20 days   | 6.15 hours             |
| 6+ years         | 25 days   | 7.69 hours             |

Accrual begins immediately but newly hired employees may not take PTO until they have
completed their first 90 days of employment.

## 4. Requesting Time Off

Employees should submit PTO requests through the Workday portal at least **ten (10)
business days** in advance for any absence of three or more consecutive days. Shorter
absences require **two (2) business days** notice. Managers will approve or decline
requests within three business days, accounting for departmental coverage.

## 5. Carryover and Year-End Balances

PTO is intended to be used within the calendar year in which it is accrued. At the close
of each calendar year, employees may **carry over a maximum of five (5) unused PTO days**
into the following year. Any balance above five days is forfeited on December 31 and is
not eligible for payout. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the new year.

## 6. Holidays

Northbridge observes eleven paid company holidays per year. Company holidays do not count
against an employee's PTO balance. A holiday falling on a weekend is observed on the
nearest business day.

## 7. Sick Leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and does not draw down the PTO balance.
Employees accrue one hour of sick leave for every 30 hours worked, up to a cap of 56
hours per year.

## 8. Separation from Employment

Upon voluntary separation with proper notice, employees are paid out for accrued but
unused PTO up to the carryover cap. The company reserves the right to amend this policy
with reasonable notice to employees.
