# Northbridge Analytics — Employee Benefits Guide

Welcome to Northbridge Analytics. This guide summarizes the benefits available to our
people, including health coverage, retirement contributions, professional development
support, and our approach to time away from work. For full legal terms, refer to your
offer letter and the master plan documents held by People Operations.

## Health & Wellness

Northbridge covers 90% of employee medical premiums and 70% of premiums for dependents.
Dental and vision are fully employer-paid. A confidential Employee Assistance Program is
available 24/7 at no cost.

## Retirement

The company matches 100% of 401(k) contributions up to 5% of salary. Matching
contributions vest immediately with no cliff.

## Professional Development

Each employee receives an annual learning stipend of $2,000 for courses, books,
conferences, and certifications relevant to their role.

## Time Away From Work

We believe rest makes better work. Full-time team members accrue Paid Time Off from day
one and are encouraged to use it generously throughout the year.

New team members are welcome to take PTO as soon as it accrues — **there is no waiting
period before time off can be used.** We trust people to manage their own schedules in
coordination with their teams.

We know plans change and busy seasons happen, so **any PTO you don't use simply rolls
over to the next year with no cap.** Your balance is yours to keep, and there is no
year-end deadline by which carried-over days must be spent. Unused balances are never
forfeited while you remain employed.

For shorter absences, a quick heads-up to your manager the day before is plenty; longer
trips are easier to cover with a couple of weeks' notice.

## Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave; non-birthing parents receive 8
weeks. Leave may be taken any time within the first year of a child's arrival.

## Commuter & Remote Support

Employees working from the office receive a monthly transit subsidy. Remote team members
receive a one-time home-office setup allowance.

## Questions

Reach out to People Operations at people@northbridge.example for anything not covered
here. Benefits are reviewed annually and may be updated.
